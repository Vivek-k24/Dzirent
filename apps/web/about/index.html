About dzirent

Good style deserves
another occasion.

Dzirent connects people who want to share their wardrobes with people looking for something to wear nearby.

For renters

Discover local outfits, view photos and details, and contact the person who owns the garment.

For lenders

List clothing from your own wardrobe, manage availability and talk directly with interested renters.

Dzirent is a peer-to-peer connection service. Payments, shipping and platform-managed reservations are not part of the current Android pilot.
